CFD (ANSYS Fluent)

Open in SpaceClaim or Discovery, use Fill to make a surface, then name the edges by layer: INLET, WALL, AXIS, OUTLET, FARFIELD.

Named zones inlet, wall and outlet for Fluent Meshing (watertight workflow, units mm).

Boundary conditions, gas properties, solver settings and the 1-D predictions to check against.

In Fluent: Results › Plots › XY Plot › Load File, to overlay the 1-D model on your CFD result.

Model: steady quasi-one-dimensional flow of a calorically perfect gas with isentropic branches and at most one normal shock. It does not model boundary layers, flow separation, oblique shocks, real-gas effects or condensation. Flow outside the nozzle is drawn schematically. Use it to learn the trends, and use experiments to learn how far real nozzles depart from them.
